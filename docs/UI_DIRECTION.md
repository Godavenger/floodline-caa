## Subject and data inventory

- Three synthetic wards with nine analysis cells and stable WGS84 geometries.
- Rainfall is an interval amount in mm, accompanied by receipt and observation time.
- Gauge levels use a declared local demo datum; they are not road-water depths.
- A selected ward has a risk word, relative index, data support and reasons.
- Pending reports remain private; verified observations are public evidence.
- Directed road segments are excluded only by active verified hazards.
- Personal alerts are consent based and expire; a failed send remains visible.
- Demo time and the synthetic mode are prominent and consistent across screens.
- The product name is Floodline, a short name for the supplied project title.
- The map and data come from the shared fixture and APIs; counts are computed.
