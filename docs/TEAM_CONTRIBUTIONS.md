case and the complete data-to-action flow.

## Individual reflection template

- What decision did I own, and what evidence supported it?
- What alternative did I consider, and why did I select this approach?
- What failed during testing, and what changed?
- Which part needs real data or human validation?
- What did I learn from another module's handoff?
