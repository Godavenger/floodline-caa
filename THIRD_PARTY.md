Python dependencies are installed from their pinned package distributions, rather than vendored in this source release. Their respective licences remain in those distributions. Optional OpenStreetMap tiles retain attribution in the map. All pilot roads, wards, environmental observations and evaluation labels are project-authored synthetic fixtures.
